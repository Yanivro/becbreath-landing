# Brief — Becoming Breath landing page

## What to build
A single, long-scroll landing page for **Bec's 1:1 breathwork sessions**. Goal:
a warm, informed visitor who books a session via WhatsApp.

- **Skill:** web prototype / landing page
- **Design system:** the uploaded `DESIGN.md` (Becoming Breath) — do not use a
  bundled system.
- **Output:** one self-contained HTML page, responsive (mobile-first; most
  traffic will come from Instagram on a phone).
- **Audience:** anyone curious about or navigating something difficult — no
  gendered split. The PDF is the source of truth, not the carousel's men/women
  slides.

## Source of truth
`breathing-with-bec-source.pdf` — all copy comes from here. Do not invent claims,
testimonials, or credentials. Preserve Bec's exact phrasing where it's quotable
("Safety… it is the work", "Welcome beautiful soul", "With love, Bec x").

## Page sections (in order)
1. **Hero** — "Becoming Breath" script wordmark, headline drawn from the PDF
   intro, hero portrait of Bec, primary WhatsApp CTA.
2. **What is Rebirthing Breathwork?** — the PDF definition; gentle, trauma-informed.
3. **About Me** — Bec's approach; the "safety is the work" idea as a torn-paper
   pull-quote. Portrait photo.
4. **What to Expect in a Session** — check-in → guided breathing journey →
   rest & integrate. Session length 1.5–2 hrs (first session ~2 hrs).
5. **Location Options** — at her space / at your home (travel fee may apply) /
   online. Three cards.
6. **Pricing** — full table, shown on the page:
   - Single session — 4,000 THB
   - Package of 3 — 3,500 THB/session (10,500 THB total)
   - Integration call (~1 hr) — 1,000 THB
   - Plus the note on integration calls (optional add-on, booked in the days after).
7. **Before We Begin** — payment (cash THB / Wise), the two intake forms,
   confidentiality, cancellation policy.
8. **Closing CTA** — "Questions? / I'm looking forward to working with you" +
   WhatsApp button. Sign-off: "With love, Bec x".
9. **Footer** — @BecBreath, the leaf/confidentiality line from the PDF.

## CTA
Primary action everywhere is **WhatsApp**. Use a placeholder link
`https://wa.me/PLACEHOLDER?text=Hi%20Bec...` until the real number is supplied.

## Assets
- `images/` — Bec's facilitation photos (hero portrait, in-session shots, the
  space, an About-Me portrait). Use honest labeled placeholders for any slot
  with no photo yet.
- `reference-carousel/` — the 6 existing Instagram slides. **Reference only**
  for visual style; do not embed them in the page.

## Constraints
- No pressure/scarcity tactics. No invented testimonials or stats.
- THB pricing exactly as above.
- Mobile layout is the priority; desktop is the enhancement.
