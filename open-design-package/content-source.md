## What Is Rebirthing Breathwork?
Rebirthing breathwork is a gentle yet profound conscious connected breathing
practice. Through the breath, we create the conditions for the body and nervous
system to release deeply held tension, emotion, and old patterns — often ones
that words alone can't reach.

Sessions are held in a safe, supportive container with a trauma-informed
approach. You don't need any previous experience, just a willingness to show up
and breathe.

## About Me
I bring a grounded, trauma-informed approach to this work, with experience
supporting clients through deep healing — from those navigating trauma and
nervous system dysregulation, to professional athletes using the breath as a
tool for performance and recovery. My practice is rooted in the science of the
body and a deep respect for what can't always be explained.

What makes my approach a little different is the container I create. Every
session is deeply personal — shaped around *you*, your history, and where you
are right now. I work slowly and gently, always led by what your body and
nervous system are ready for. There is no pushing, no performing, and no
pressure to have a particular experience.

At the heart of everything is safety. I understand that the body holds more than
words can express and that real change happens when people feel genuinely held.
Creating that environment of trust isn't something I add to the work. It *is*
the work.

If you're navigating something difficult, looking to go deeper, or simply
curious about what breathwork can offer spiritually or emotionally, you are
welcome here exactly as you are.

## What to Expect in a Session
Each session is tailored to you. In general, a session includes:
- A check-in conversation to understand where you are and set an intention
- A guided breathing journey (the main practice)
- Time to rest, integrate, and debrief after

Sessions can bring up emotions, physical sensations, or unexpected insights —
this is a normal and healthy part of the process. You will always be supported
throughout.

**Session length:** 1.5 to 2 hours (the initial session is typically closer to
2 hours).
